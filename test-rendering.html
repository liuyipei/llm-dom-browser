**Common sorting algorithms:** 1. **Bubble Sort** - Simple comparison sort - Time: $O(n^2)$ - Space: $O(1)$ 2. **Merge Sort** - Divide and conquer - Time: $O(n \log n)$ - Space: $O(n)$ 3. **Quick Sort** - Partition-based - Average: $O(n \log n)$ - Worst: $O(n^2)$ *Note: Always consider the input size when choosing an algorithm!*
